# Project Brief — Builder Swarm

## One-Sentence Pitch
Builder Swarm is an open-source agentic project launcher that turns a competition brief and rough idea into a scoped, build-ready, judge-aligned launch packet.

## Competition
SSTB Agentic AI Competition

## Deadline
May 29, 2026

## Why This Project Fits
- The project is agentic by design: multiple specialist agents collaborate on a project launch workflow.
- It uses AI for research, planning, and building.
- It is open-source friendly.
- It gives back to the community by helping future builders submit stronger competition projects.

## Target User
A solo builder or small team entering a competition, hackathon, innovation sprint, or open-source challenge.

## Core Problem
Builders often lose time in the first 48 hours: interpreting rules, scoping too broadly, picking a demo path, and assembling submission artifacts.

## Core Promise
Paste the competition details and your rough idea. Get a launch packet that tells you what to build, what to cut, how to demo it, and how to submit it.
